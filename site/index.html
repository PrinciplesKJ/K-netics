What's in each entry?

Every entry captures a single experimentally measured rate constant for a standard toehold-mediated strand displacement reaction:

  • Provenance: DOI, year, first author, confidence score (1–3)
  • Strands: full substrate, incumbent, and invader sequences (5'→3')
  • Domains: toehold and branch migration sequences + lengths
  • Features: mismatches, hairpins, clamps, modified bases, terminal G–C
  • Kinetics: k_eff (M⁻¹s⁻¹), error, leak rate, measurement method
  • Conditions: temperature, buffer, [Na⁺], [Mg²⁺]
  • Thermodynamics: ΔG of toehold and duplexes (when available)
